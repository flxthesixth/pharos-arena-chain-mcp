Signals, reasons, limitations.

Six genuine Chain MCP signals produce a reproducible risk score. A “high” result means the configured heuristic fired; it is not a claim that an address is malicious.

Clean example

LOW · 0

Positive balance, lifetime nonce 8, no suspicious approvals or configured flagged interactions in the inspected window.

Risky example

HIGH · 3

Freshly funded EOA: positive balance, one incoming transfer, zero lifetime outgoing nonce, and no outgoing transfer in the inspected block.

Six explained signals

Contract bytecode, native balance, lifetime nonce, recent activity, approval pattern, and explicitly configured flagged-address interactions.

Limits

Chain MCP exposes bytecode presence, not explorer source verification. Recent-window absence never means an event never happened.
